We build movable buildings and the systems around them, from Tivat, Montenegro.

The group

Korsakov Group started with a single question: what does a home look like when it doesn't need land, a mortgage, or a permit that takes three years?

The answer turned into a family of businesses. Some make buildings, some make the software that designs them, some keep the crafts that furnish them alive. Each runs on its own, and each one feeds the others.
